How it works

Half to start, half when your site goes live. Bank transfer or Revolut.

  1. Free preview

    I make a preview of your homepage. No cost, no commitment.

  2. Agreement and 50% deposit

    You sign a short agreement and pay half to get started.

  3. Send your content

    Your logo, photos, menu or services, prices and opening hours.

  4. Build and changes

    I build the site and you review it within your included rounds.

  5. Launch

    You pay the other 50%, and I connect your domain and put it live.

  6. After launch

    Bug fixes are free for 30 days, then a care plan or the hourly rate.
